# Data availability and research ethics clarification

This public repository provides the **Paper 1 authentic-only source-discrimination code** and **non-identifying aggregate results**. The original experiment was performed using authentic face crops derived from publicly accessible video clips of 18 Ghanaian public figures and authentic FaceForensics++ (FF++) images. The separate cross-source baseline also used manipulated FF++ images.

**The image dataset itself is not hosted or downloadable in this repository.** Public visibility of the original videos does not by itself establish redistribution rights for extracted facial crops, and research access to FF++ is subject to its terms. The existing data-access restrictions have not been changed by publishing the code.

FaceForensics++ original dataset information: https://github.com/ondyari/FaceForensics

The recorded source-discrimination results are available at `results/test_metrics.json` and `results/training_history.csv`. The original `source_discrimination.py` script is available in this repository. The original split manifest, subject-linked predictions, third-party image files, and research videos have not been released.

This repository does **not** assert that institutional ethics approval or individual informed consent was obtained. The authors must address any editorial ethics requirements accurately with their institution and the journal, and must not describe an approval, waiver, exemption, or consent as obtained without records.
